# ActionBarAnything

Put **any** bag item on your action bars — even the ones the game refuses — and see how many you are carrying right there.

World of Warcraft only lets you drop *usable* or *equippable* items onto an action bar. Leather, ore, cloth, eggs, quest drops: refused. ActionBarAnything lifts that restriction. Drag Heavy Leather (or anything else) from your bags onto an empty action bar slot and the slot shows the item's icon with a live count of how many you have in your bags, top-left, exactly like a stack of potions would.

Handy for keeping an eye on gathering mats, quest collectibles or vendor trash without opening your bags.

## Using it

- **Drag** an item from your bags onto an empty action bar slot. A blue highlight follows your mouse over the slots that will take it.
- **Hover** the slot for the item tooltip.
- **Drag** it to another slot to move it, or to a bag to take it off the bar.
- **Right-click** the slot to clear it.
- Placements are saved **per character** and follow the slot through bar paging and stances.
- Blizzard's **Lock Action Bars** setting is honoured: when locked, hold the Pick Up Action key (Shift by default) to drag an item off.
- Dropping a spell, macro or usable item onto one of these slots works as normal and replaces it.

These slots are for looking, not clicking: the game still considers the slot empty, so its keybind does nothing and the item cannot be used from the bar.

## Options

`Esc` > Options > AddOns > ActionBarAnything (or `/aba`): an enable toggle. Turning it off hides the items and restores stock drag behaviour; your placements are kept.

## Compatibility

Works with Blizzard's default action bars (all eight bars). Supports WoW Forever and Classic Era. No libraries.
